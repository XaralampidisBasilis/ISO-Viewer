    so that in the CPU you can use new Uint16Array(this.tensor.dataSync().buffer) to get directly the conversion.

2. You can use a Compander, in order to compress the interpolation map into Uint8 texture and have a 1D expander map 
    to translate the Uint8 values into the correct float normalized values. So specifically do a non uniform quantization based
    on the histogram of the occupied voxels.
