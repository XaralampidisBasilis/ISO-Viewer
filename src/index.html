IEEE BIBE 2025

High-Quality Analytic Isosurface Rendering for Web-Based Cardiac Imaging

Vasileios Lazaros Charalampidis and colleagues presented these methods for cardiac imaging. The viewer applies them to a head-and-neck CT angiogram.

Implicit surfaces
Each pixel solves for a ray’s first intersection with the chosen intensity level.
Distance maps
A grid of block bounds and distances lets rays skip regions that cannot contain the surface.
Bernstein rejection
Polynomial coefficients identify cells the ray can pass through without a root solve.
